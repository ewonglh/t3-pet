Stack and motion demo

Hover the cards, then press Up or Down.

Card enters
Drops in from 12px above at 96% scale, fades in over 160ms, settles in 240ms (--ease-out). The older cards shift back one step at the same time.
Stack shuffle
Down lifts the front card 14px and fades it out; the next card rises to the front. Up reverses it. Each step is one transform and one opacity transition, so it stays smooth.
Dismiss
The card fades and lifts 8px; cards behind move up. The count and badge update straight away.
Depth
Each level back moves 9px down, shrinks 4.5% and fades 30%. Back cards show only their glass edge, so text never ghosts through.
Hover controls
Dismiss badge scales in from 80%; time cross-fades to "Open" in 160ms.
Reduced motion
Movement durations drop to 0ms and only 120ms fades remain, from the OS setting or the toggle here. Nothing moves, but changes are still visible.
